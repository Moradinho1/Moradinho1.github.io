Selected project

Advanced Excel — Business Data Analysis & Transportation Optimization

A multi-sheet academic model combining business-rule calculations, dynamic reporting and a constrained transportation optimization problem.

Excel · Operations Research · Data Analysis

From raw operational data to an optimized shipping plan

The workbook processes sales and logistics data, applies tax and pricing rules, converts currencies by date, builds dynamic filtered outputs and uses Solver to minimize vehicle transportation cost across ports and destination cities.

XLOOKUP / lookup logicDynamic FILTERData ValidationConditional FormattingBusiness RulesSolverSimplex LP

What the model handles

Business-rule calculationsVAT and customs by country, conditional discounts, payment totals, tiered agent commissions and port-storage costs.
Date-aware foreign exchangeConverts transaction values using the relevant exchange-rate table while accounting for dates without FX trading.
Dynamic reportingDropdown-driven selection of currency, country and sales agent feeds a single dynamic report that returns matching records.
Transportation optimizationAllocates vehicles from Haifa, Ashdod and Eilat ports to Tel Aviv, Jerusalem and Be’er Sheva while respecting supply and exact-demand constraints.
